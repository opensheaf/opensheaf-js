# OCFL 1.1 fixtures

The objects of `1.1/good-objects`, `1.1/bad-objects` and `1.1/warn-objects`
are copied unchanged from the fixtures of the OCFL editors,
<https://github.com/OCFL/fixtures>, commit
`d323fc207126becec066998b43408d998bf0fb1b` (5 December 2024), for the tests
of the OCFL validator (chapter 77 of the literate program). They are
published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(CC BY 4.0, <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>), as the
fixtures declare in their `.zenodo.json`: "Oxford Common File Layout -
Fixtures", by the OCFL editors.

The files are kept byte for byte (`.gitattributes`: no conversion of line
ends), since their digests are recorded in the inventories.
